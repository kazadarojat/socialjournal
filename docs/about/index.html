Journal profile

Fronstellar Journal of Integrative Multidisciplinary Social Sciences is published by Fronstellar Institute and focuses on integrative social sciences. It is being prepared to connect researchers, scholars, and practitioners studying society through relationships between fields of knowledge.

Article languages are Indonesian and English. Publication is planned every four months, equivalent to three issues per year. Publication months and dates will be announced after confirmation.

Aims of the journal

The journal aims to develop scholarly conversations beyond isolated readings of social problems. Integration involves a reasoned connection between concepts, theories, methods, or findings across perspectives, rather than merely collecting different topics in one paper.

  • Encourage context-sensitive research on society and social change.
  • Connect theoretical and methodological traditions around clear research questions.
  • Relate academic findings to reflection on social practice and policy.
  • Support scholarly exchange in Indonesian and English.

Aims & scope

The journal publishes integrative social sciences research across the following areas:

  • Sociology and anthropology: social relations, communities, identity, culture, and change.
  • Politics and public policy: governance, institutions, participation, and power.
  • Education and communication: learning, media, literacy, and knowledge production.
  • Economics and development in social context: welfare, inequality, work, and livelihoods.
  • Cross-disciplinary studies: gender, environment, technology, religion, and other social questions with clear connections across perspectives.

Scope fit is assessed through the research question, contribution, and depth of analysis.

Article types

  • Research articles: empirical studies with traceable methods and findings.
  • Conceptual articles: development or critique of ideas, theoretical frameworks, and conceptual relationships.
  • Review articles: synthesis of literature with an explained search and analytical approach.

Readership & languages

The journal is intended for researchers, lecturers, students, practitioners, and policy readers interested in cross-disciplinary social inquiry. Articles use Indonesian or English consistently. Bilingual abstracts are proposed to broaden accessibility.

Publication & access

Published by Fronstellar Institute three times per year, at four-month intervals. Specific issue dates will be announced by the editorial team. Editor in Chief: Yoga Hediasa, M.Hum.

The journal adopts open access and Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). There are no submission, processing, or publication charges. No ISSN, DOI, accreditation, or indexing status is claimed until verified.

The first manuscript is available for review. No issue has been formally published; the sample manuscript is not presented as an accepted or peer-reviewed article.
